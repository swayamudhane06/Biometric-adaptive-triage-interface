# Biometric-adaptive-triage-interface

Week 1 — Research & Project Understanding

During Week 1, I focused on understanding the concept and requirements of the Biometric-Adaptive Triage Interface. I researched the triage process and studied how biometric and vital health parameters such as heart rate, blood pressure, SpO₂, respiratory rate and temperature can support initial patient assessment.
I also researched existing digital triage and patient-monitoring systems to understand their workflows, features and user interfaces. I identified the primary users of the system, including patients and medical staff, and studied their basic needs and pain points. Based on the research, I defined the initial project objectives, requirements and possible features/screens for the application.

 Week 2 — User Flow, Wireframing & UI Planning

During Week 2, I converted the research findings into the initial structure of the application. I created and planned the user flows for patients and medical staff, including login/register, patient information, biometric data collection and triage assessment.
I planned the main screens and started creating low-fidelity wireframes. I worked on the layout, navigation, information hierarchy, cards, buttons and presentation of vital information and severity levels. I also explored different UI approaches for displaying patient data and alerts clearly, with the goal of making the interface simple and easy to use in an emergency healthcare environment.
These wireframes and flow structures will be used as the foundation for the next stage of high-fidelity UI design and interactive prototyping.
